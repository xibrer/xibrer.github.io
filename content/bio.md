I am currently a first-year PhD student at Beihang University. I earned my M.S. in Mathematics from Beijing University of Technology, and hold a Bachelor's degree from Shenyang Aerospace University. My research interests lie in AIoT, smartphone-based healthcare sensing, AI-augmented audio perception, and millimeter-wave (mmWave) sensing.

I have published three student first-author and one co-first-author papers in ACM IMWUT (UbiComp) and IEEE INFOCOM on smartphone-based healthcare sensing and audio enhancement.
